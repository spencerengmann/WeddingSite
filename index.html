A little guest guidance

When should I RSVP?

Please RSVP by October 15, 2026, and select every event you will attend. Include each guest’s full name, including children and invited plus-ones.

Can I bring a plus-one?

Only invited, RSVP-listed guests can attend. Any plus-one must be included in your RSVP. Unregistered guests will not be admitted or have a seat. No exceptions.

Are children welcome?

Yes. Children must be included in your RSVP and remain with their parent or guardian at all times. Please do not let children roam around the building unsupervised.

How do I find my table?

Table assignments will be provided at the wedding venue. Please include every invited guest in your RSVP so we can plan your places.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive 15 minutes before each event. See the schedule above for the arrival times and locations.
